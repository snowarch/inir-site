Already on iNiR?

2.32.0 turns iRiS into a desktop you shape by touching it: click the Island, the Dock, a bubble or a widget and its options grow right there, widgets stack and read the wallpaper under them, the Control Center and the lock screen are yours to arrange, Orbit lays your whole session out from the Island, and the shell and your apps switch together between dark, ink and light.

  • Customize where you look

    Click the Island, the Dock, a bubble or a piece and its options grow out of it, with Themes, Look, Pieces, undo and Done on the Island and a ring that follows the shape you are editing.

    Scheme in Appearance follows the system or stays where you put it.

  • Colour themes for the shell and your apps

  • One design for every widget

    iRiS, Material, iNstrument or Readout from Settings or any widget's Look page, with Match for the ones that kept their own look and Undo for your old mix.

More about iRiS

iRiS, the Island family.

The newest family and the one moving fastest. One shape on a screen edge answers what is happening and becomes whatever you open. Every part of it has a command, so you can bind it or script it.

Any edge

Put the Island on the top, bottom, left or right. On a side it stands up with a stacked clock. The Dock sits opposite, and sending one to the other's edge makes them trade places.

Click an edge of the screen to move it there.

Dark, Ink and Light

Ink is washi and sumi, softer than white. The scheme is the system's mode, so GTK, Qt, terminals and VS Code switch with the shell, and widgets turn to dark ink where the wallpaper under them is bright.

Theming in detail

Install it.

iNiR needs Qt 6.9 or newer. The installer checks yours first and says so if it is older, instead of failing halfway.

  • ArchThe primary target
  • FedoraAutomated, 43 or newer
  • DebianAutomated, testing or newer
  • UbuntuAutomated, 25.10 or newer
  • NixOSAn experimental flake

Not for low-spec machines. You can strip it down a lot though: turn off effects, drop panels, flatten the design.

  1. git clone https://github.com/snowarch/inir.git
  2. cd inir
  3. ./setup install

The installer asks before each step. When it finishes, restart and pick Niri at the login screen. Installing in detail
