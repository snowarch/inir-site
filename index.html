2.33.0
iRiS 0.3Oct 8, 2026

Void Linux support, the iRiS lock on the login screen, and a washi palette for iRiS.

  • Void LinuxInstall, update and repair with XBPS; iNiR runs under runit or Turnstile where there is no systemd user manager.
  • The iRiS lock on the login screenSDDM in three styles, Cover, Frame and Lens, under Login Screen in Settings or with inir loginScreen style <cover|frame|lens>.
  • Washi palettePaper palettes and paper levels for iRiS and your apps, with an Afterglow texture, in Appearance.
  • Wallpaper scalingFill, fit, center, or one picture across every screen.
  • Game performance in Floating ToolsYour game's FPS in a floating overlay.
  • Update noticesWhen a new iNiR is out, a notice says so, also right after login, and Not now brings it back after the interval set in iRiS Settings › Sources.
2.32.0iRiS 0.2 · Sep 29, 2026

2.32.0 turns iRiS into a desktop you shape by touching it: click the Island, the Dock, a bubble or a widget and its options grow right there, widgets stack and read the wallpaper under them, the Control Center and the lock screen are yours to arrange, Orbit lays your whole session out from the Island, and the shell and your apps switch together between dark, ink and light.

2.31.0Sep 19, 2026

2.31.0 introduces iRiS, the third panel family, together with its Island, Themes, Studio, edge-aware Dock and desktop pieces. The release also folds in the wallpaper, widget, Settings, runtime and installer work completed on prerelease since 2.30.0.
